=============================================

Self-referential prompts cause geometric contraction in transformer
representations, measured by R_V, the ratio of late- to early-layer effective
dimensionality. Base Mistral-7B-v0.1 shows strong contraction (d = -1.85,
n_self=96, n_base=100). Dual-layer ablation confirms necessity: recursive
behavioral markers drop from 44.7% to 0.0% (session d = 2.71, n=300 per
condition). Path patching localizes the causal site to the early residual
stream (L5, d = 4.14). Cross-architecture evidence is mixed: contraction in
Mistral and Qwen, sign reversal in OPT and GPT-2 XL, null in Pythia-1.4B.
R_V < 0.737 detects self-referential content with AUROC = 0.909.

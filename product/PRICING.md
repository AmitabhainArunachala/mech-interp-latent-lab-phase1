## Pricing Rationale

### Why $29 for Tier 1?
- Low enough for impulse buy ($< a month of Netflix)
- High enough to signal quality (not a free toy)
- One-time = no commitment anxiety
- Comparable to other premium ClawHub skills

### Why $399 for Tier 2?
- 1 hour of expert time ($50) + analysis work ($50) + tool setup
- Value proposition: save $100-300/month in wasted API costs
- ROI positive within 2-4 months for target customer
- Positions as professional service, not just software

### Why $149/month for Tier 3?
- Less than cost of one wasted agent running 24/7 (~$200/month)
- Quarterly audit alone worth ~$400 (essentially 1 audit/quarter included)
- Priority support justifies recurring commitment
- Annual contract option at $1,490/year ($124/mo — 2 months free)
